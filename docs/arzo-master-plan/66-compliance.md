## Licence — the live finding

### What the licence says

`LICENCE` opens with a term added under AGPL Section 7(b), before the standard text:

> In accordance with Section 7(b) of the AGPL, you are required to retain the "Powered by Hi.Events"
> attribution at the footer of all web pages and emails generated by this software. If you modify
> Hi.Events, you may rephrase the attribution to reflect your changes, for example, "Powered by [Your
> Company] based on Hi.Events," but the link must always direct to https://hi.events. If you wish to
> remove this attribution, a commercial license is available. (`LICENCE:1-8`)

The same term is restated in `PoweredByFooter/index.tsx` (header comment) and in the email footer
template (`resources/views/vendor/mail/html/message.blade.php:31-36`). `98` analyses the terms and the
published commercial tiers and recommends buying the Platform licence; `120` R1 records the risk.
This section holds the evidence of the live defect, ARZ-317: rebranding is permitted only in the
rephrased form, or with a commercial licence.

### What the working tree does

The rebrand is uncommitted, in progress at audit time.

| Change | Effect | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Web footer text becomes "Powered by ARZO, based on Hi.Events", link unchanged | `PoweredByFooter/index.tsx:60` | The licence's own permitted form |
| Email footer becomes "Powered by ARZO, based on Hi.Events", link to hi.events | `message.blade.php:38` | Permitted form |
| **`frontend/.env.example` gains `VITE_I_HAVE_PURCHASED_A_LICENCE=false`** (line 19; absent at baseline); the gitignored `frontend/.env` has the same line | `iHavePurchasedALicence()` returns the raw string (`utilites/helpers.ts:160-162`, `utilites/config.ts:32-40`). The string `"false"` is truthy, so `PoweredByFooter` returns an empty fragment (`index.tsx:24-26`) | **Removes the attribution from every page that renders it** |

**Verified, not inferred.** On the running dev stack, the server-rendered HTML of
`/event/1/bootstrap-single-event` (fetched 2026-09-29) carries
`"VITE_I_HAVE_PURCHASED_A_LICENCE":"false"` in `window.hievents` — `server.js:12` loads `.env` with
`dotenv`, and `server.js:98-106` exposes every `VITE_` variable — and contains **no "Powered by"
text anywhere**. Fifteen components render the footer, among them the event and organizer homepages, the
checkout steps, tickets, "my tickets", print views, error pages and the auth layout.

The email footer still carries the attribution in the dev stack. It can be removed entirely by
setting `APP_EMAIL_FOOTER_TEXT`, an upstream option that replaces the whole footer
(`message.blade.php:28-30`); it is not set in dev.

### What is not established

- **Whether ARZO holds a commercial licence from Hi.Events.** If it does, removal is permitted and
  the flag should be set deliberately, with the licence recorded in the compliance register below.
  `UNVERIFIED` — the business must answer.
- **Whether the effect is intentional.** The line reads as "I have *not* purchased a licence"; the
  code reads it as the opposite. It may be an accident of the string-typed config (C2).
- **Production.** Whether the production frontend is built or run from this env file is
  `UNVERIFIED`.
- **Enforceability.** Whether a footer-on-every-page requirement is a valid §7(b) term or a
  "further restriction" a recipient may remove (`LICENCE:377-385`) is a legal question —
  `UNVERIFIED`, and `98` reaches the same view. This plan treats the term as binding: the copyright
  holder states it plainly, and disputing it is a worse commercial position than keeping a one-line
  footer or buying the licence.

### Why it is critical

Under Section 8 (`LICENCE:396-422`), propagating or modifying a covered work outside the licence's
terms terminates the rights it grants. Rights are reinstated if the violation stops — permanently,
if the copyright holder does not object within 60 days, or on a first notice cured within 30 days.
If this configuration ships, ARZO runs a modified AGPL program without the conditions it is licensed
under. The fix costs one line; the exposure is the right to run the platform at all.

### AGPL Section 13 — the source offer

Section 13 (`LICENCE:541-552`) requires a modified version to **prominently offer all users
interacting with it remotely** access to its Corresponding Source. ARZO's version is modified.

- No page offers ARZO's source. The only licence links are code comments; the "About & Support"
  modal frames hi.events (`modals/AboutModal/index.tsx:9`), which offers upstream's source, not
  ARZO's changes.
- The local repository's `origin` is `github.com/HiEventsDev/Hi.Events`; whether ARZO's modifications
  are published anywhere is `UNVERIFIED`.
- The same truthy-string defect hides the About link: `VITE_HIDE_ABOUT_LINK=false` in the working
  tree's `.env.example:18` is read as "hide" (`GlobalMenu/index.tsx:67`).

This is R1 made concrete. Every ARZO subsystem built in this codebase is part of the Corresponding
Source unless the commercial licence is taken. A **private** ARZO repository (ARZ-300) does not
conflict with Section 13 on its own — the obligation is to offer the source to users, not to host it
publicly — but without the commercial licence, every user of the service must be offered it.

## Card data — PCI DSS scope

The design choice `15` asks to protect holds: card data is entered in Stripe-hosted fields and never
touches ARZO servers, logs or databases. Offline card payment stays excluded (`71`).

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| SAQ level | **SAQ A is the expected level** for an integration where all card fields are in a PCI-compliant provider's iframe. `UNVERIFIED` — the acquirer or a QSA confirms it, and PCI DSS v4's expectations for scripts on pages that host payment iframes must be checked against the current SAQ A text |
| Who validates | In SaaS mode each organizer is the merchant on its own connected account; outside SaaS mode ARZO is. See merchant of record |
| Third-party scripts on the payment page | Organizer pixels load on the checkout layout (`Checkout/index.tsx:253`). **Recommendation:** no organizer or marketing scripts on the payment step. Conversion tracking fires on the confirmation page |
| Stripe.js source | Loaded through `loadStripe` from Stripe, not bundled (`Payment/PaymentMethods/Stripe/index.tsx:41`) — keep it that way |
| Webhook integrity | Verify the signature before acknowledging (`49`, `64`) |

## Merchant of record

`15` left this open. The code answers half of it: in SaaS mode, charges are **direct charges** on the
organizer's connected account — Stripe describes these as customers transacting directly with the
connected account, the charge appearing on its balance
(<https://docs.stripe.com/connect/direct-charges>, accessed 2026-09-29). Outside SaaS mode, ARZO's own
account takes the charge.

`saas_mode_enabled` is **global**, and in SaaS mode an organizer without a connected account cannot
take payment (`StripePaymentIntentCreationService.php:128-137`). A mixed model — ARZO selling its own
events as merchant while external organizers use Connect — works only if ARZO connects its own
account to its own platform like any organizer. That is workable and should be the documented setup.

What follows from being merchant — chargeback liability, consumer-law refund duties, tax
registration — is `UNVERIFIED` and belongs to ARZO's finance and legal leads.

## Tax and e-invoicing

- **EU VAT:** VIES validation and VAT settings exist (`15`), relevant only to EU-established
  organizers and buyers.
- **Qatar VAT:** secondary sources report no VAT in force as of mid-2026 and an expected rate of 5%
  (for example <https://www.taxinme.com/articles/qatar-vat-status-2026.php>, accessed 2026-09-29).
  Not confirmed from a primary source — `UNVERIFIED`; the General Tax Authority or a tax adviser must
  confirm.
- **Qatar e-invoicing:** Qatar News Agency reports the Cabinet **approved a draft e-invoicing law and
  its executive regulations** on 6 May 2026
  (<https://qna.org.qa/en/news/news-details?id=cabinet-holds-regular-meeting&date=6%2F05%2F2026>,
  accessed 2026-09-29). Its status since, scope, format and start date are `UNVERIFIED`.

Consequence for the platform: invoices today are dompdf PDFs generated from orders (`15`). If Qatari
e-invoicing requires structured, reported or cleared invoices, the invoice service needs a second
output format and possibly a reporting integration. Treat it as a **watch item with a named owner**,
not as work to start.

## Accessibility — a standard by decision, not by statute

No Qatari statute found imposes a web or kiosk accessibility standard. ARZO should adopt **WCAG 2.2
AA** for public pages, checkout and kiosks anyway: government clients commonly require it in
procurement (`UNVERIFIED` per client), attendees at public events include disabled people, and the
theming system already enforces contrast (`02`). Kiosk obligations are R20; the audit and the
component work belong to `81`. The standard is ARZO's commitment, and it is recorded as such, not
asserted as law.

## Third-party assets in the working tree

The rebrand self-hosts **SF Pro Display** (`frontend/public/fonts/sf/*.woff2`, preloaded in
`index.html`; uncommitted at audit time). Apple distributes the San Francisco fonts under their own
licence agreement; Apple's Design Resources licence says so (§1.B) and restricts design resources to
mock-ups for Apple platforms
(<https://developer.apple.com/support/downloads/terms/apple-design-resources/Apple-Design-Resources-License-20230621-English.pdf>,
accessed 2026-09-29). Whether the font licence itself permits serving the font on a public website is
`UNVERIFIED` — it was not read — and public reports say it does not. Resolve before the font is
committed; a licensed or open alternative is cheap.
