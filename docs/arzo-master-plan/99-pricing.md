## Purpose

How the product is priced.

## Current state

`PARTIAL`: per-transaction application fees in SaaS mode.

## Key decisions

- On-site capabilities (badges, access control, devices) are value-priced per event, not per ticket — the cost structure is entirely different.
- Hardware and staffing, if offered, are cost-plus, not SaaS margin.

## Open questions

- Per-event, per-attendee, or subscription for the on-site suite?
- Is hardware rented, sold, or bundled?
