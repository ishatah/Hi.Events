## Purpose

Positioning, buyer, and the build-vs-partner line for each capability area.

## Current state

ARZO today is self-serve ticketing software. It has no on-site service offering, no hardware, and no staffing arm. `CONFIRMED` from the audit: strong commerce, absent on-site operations.

## Key decisions

- Two viable positions: (a) remain a ticketing product and partner for on-site accreditation; (b) become a full on-site suite, which requires hardware and staff. This plan documents (b) while stating (a)'s lower cost.
- ARZO operates its own events in Qatar, so the platform must serve an internal operator as well as external SaaS tenants. That dual role shapes tenancy (`08`) and commercial model (`98`).

## Open questions

- Is the primary buyer ARZO's own operations team, or external organizers? The answer changes UX priorities and the SaaS packaging in `100`.
- Which capabilities are genuinely differentiating vs table stakes? Informs `112`.
- Build, buy, or partner for RFID hardware and badge stock?
