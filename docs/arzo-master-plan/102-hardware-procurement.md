## Purpose

What must be bought, and the cost envelope.

## Current state

**Out of engineering scope — a business commitment.** Documented here because Phases 2 and 4 are not deliverable without it.

## Key decisions

- Indicative per-event kit: badge printers plus spares, badge stock, lanyards and holders, handheld or tablet scanners, kiosk units with enclosures, RFID readers and cards if in scope, networking and power, charging and storage.
- Consumables (badge stock, ribbons, lanyards) are recurring cost per event, not capex.
- Spares are not optional: a printer failing at 08:00 with a queue forming is a business risk, not an inconvenience.
- Procurement lead times — especially printers and RFID — can exceed the software timeline. Order before Phase 4, not during.

## Open questions

- Buy or rent? Renting suits irregular event calendars.
- Vendor selection drives `37`-`39` adapter work, so choose early enough to inform engineering.
- What is the actual budget envelope? This plan cannot estimate it without a target event profile.
